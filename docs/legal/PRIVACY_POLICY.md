# Privacy Policy – RICS Twitch Extension

**Last updated:** 2026-08-08

This Privacy Policy applies to the **Rimworld Interactive Chat Service Hub** Twitch Extension (the “Extension”), which works with the Rimworld Interactive Chat Services (RICS) mod for RimWorld.

## What we collect

**We do not collect personal information.**

The Extension does not collect, store, or sell:
- Names, email addresses, or contact details
- Payment information
- Location data
- Device identifiers beyond what Twitch already provides to Extensions
- Browsing history or analytics profiles

## What Twitch provides

Like all Twitch Extensions, the Extension receives limited information from Twitch that is necessary for it to function (for example, an opaque user identifier, channel information, and role such as viewer / moderator / broadcaster). This information is provided by Twitch under Twitch’s own policies and is used only to operate the Extension (identify the correct viewer’s pawn, respect permissions, etc.).

We do not use this information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to running the Extension and the linked RICS mod.

## Data handled by RICS

Game-related data (pawn assignment, balances, purchases, etc.) is handled by the RICS mod running on the streamer’s computer and any backend the streamer chooses to run. That data stays under the streamer’s control and is subject to how the streamer operates their channel and mod.

## Third parties

The Extension runs inside Twitch. Use of Twitch is governed by Twitch’s Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. We do not send your data to other third-party analytics or advertising services.

## Children

The Extension is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the Extension after changes means you accept the updated policy.
